# Code of conduct

Treat contributors, users and maintainers with respect. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, publication of private information and disruptive
conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report incidents privately to [joshua@pondsec.com](mailto:joshua@pondsec.com).
Reports are handled discreetly and may result in a warning, removal of content or
loss of participation privileges. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome
when it remains focused on the work.

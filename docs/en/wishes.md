# Wishes and projects

[Deutsch](../de/wishes.md) · [Documentation](../INDEX.md)

**Wishes & projects** keeps planned purchases separate from owned inventory. It works with any kind of item, including tools, furniture, collections, equipment and the parts of a build project.

## Add an individual wish

1. Open **Wishes & projects → Add wish**.
2. Enter a name and desired quantity. Price, priority, research, source link, category and model are optional.
3. Choose visibility. **Private** is visible only to its creator; **Shared with the team** is visible to authorized users of the instance.
4. Save. The wish does not appear in inventory yet.
5. When you acquire one unit, click **I have this**. It becomes an individual asset and the missing quantity decreases by one.

The arrow beside **I have this** opens acquisition options. You can enter up to 50 units at once, an actual unit cost, location, an individual serial number and category properties. An individual serial number belongs to one unit. Required category properties must be completed before acquisition.

Use **Use an existing inventory asset** to find and link an owned asset without creating a duplicate. An asset can fulfil only one wish at a time and cannot already belong to another assembly.

## Put a project together

1. Select **Create project** and enter a goal, optional budget and target date.
2. Add items. Each item is a wish with its own quantity, research and price.
3. Acquire units over time or link assets already in inventory.
4. Review progress, missing quantities, planned costs and recorded costs. Missing prices mean totals are incomplete. All items use the project currency; amounts are not converted automatically.
5. Once all active items are acquired, select **Assemble as an asset**. This creates a parent asset containing the acquired assets. The items remain individually inventoried.

After assembly, edit the actual contents on the asset page. The project plan remains as a reference. Archived wishes do not count towards the required project items.

## Correct and organize

Click a wish name to open, edit or archive it. **Unlink** reverses an accidental acquisition association; the actual asset stays in inventory. Deleting a linked asset from inventory makes that quantity missing again.

Deleting a wish removes its research, not its acquired assets. Deleting a project keeps its wishes private to their respective creators. An assembled asset also remains in inventory.

## Permissions and backup

**View wishes and projects** and **Manage wishes and projects** apply to your own plans and shared plans. They do not expose other users' private plans. Acquisition and assembly also require the appropriate asset management permissions. An account with linked plans or project history cannot be deleted until those records have been resolved.

Full database backups include wishes, projects and acquisition history. Portable inventory exports include the actual assets, but not private wish lists. Back up the database and upload directory together to restore product images as well.
